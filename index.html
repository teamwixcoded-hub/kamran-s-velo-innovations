    What working with us looks like

    Four steps. You always know what happens next.

    1. Step 1

      Discovery

      We start with a call. You tell us what you need, we ask a lot of questions, and then we give you a clear scope and price.

    2. Step 2

      Plan

      Before anything gets built, we agree on the pages, how the data will work and the milestones.

    3. Step 3

      Build

      We build in stages and send you a private link at each milestone, so you can look around and tell us what to change.

    4. Step 4

      Launch & optimise

      We test everything before going live, hand it all over to you, and tune speed and SEO once real visitors arrive.
