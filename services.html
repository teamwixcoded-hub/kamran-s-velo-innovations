FAQ

Common questions

How long does a project take?

Timelines are listed on each package and confirmed in your written quote after the discovery call. They depend on scope and how quickly feedback arrives.

How many revisions are included?

Each milestone includes a review round. The number of rounds is stated in your quote. Additional work is billed at the hourly rate or agreed in advance.

Who owns the site and the code?

You do. Ownership transfers on final payment, and we hand over access to everything we built. See our Terms of Service.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in defined cases. Deposits are refundable before work starts, and unstarted milestones are refundable. Full details are in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
